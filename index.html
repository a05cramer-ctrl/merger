How a merge works.

Same rules for every merge. Holders never sign anything.

  1. 01 · SNAPSHOT

    Both coins, one slot.

    Every holder of coin A and coin B is read at the same block. No time to buy in after the merge is called.

  2. 02 · ZERO

    Devs and the pool get nothing.

    The creator wallets and the curve's own vault are cut. Wallets worth under $1 are dropped as dust.

  3. 03 · WEIGH

    Your bags, added up in dollars.

    Your share is your USD value in A + B. Hold both, you get both. No wallet gets more than 3% of the airdrop.

  4. 04 · AIRDROP

    One new coin on StonkFun.

    The merger wallet buys 5% of the new coin at launch, in the same transaction. When it graduates, that 5% is airdropped to the holders. Nothing to claim.
